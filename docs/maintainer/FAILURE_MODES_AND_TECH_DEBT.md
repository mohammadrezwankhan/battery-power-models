## Accepted limitations and unresolved delivery facts

An ideal first-order thermal model and ideal-capacitor equations are deliberate reference scope, not automatically bugs. Omitted spatial/electrochemical/control effects belong to explicit future scope decisions. [Assumptions](../math-and-physics/ASSUMPTIONS_AND_LIMITS.md) records them.

The classic-protection API permission limitation, candidate CI, review status and new deployment proof are operational facts to resolve during delivery, not excuses to weaken requirements. The local Chromium launch limitation prevents a local visual-pass claim; static checks are useful but do not replace independent browser inspection. Full scientific/application sources were reviewed; selected scanner internals and generated assets remain indexed with reasons in [coverage](../SOURCE_EVIDENCE.md).

The [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md) maps these findings to measurable proposals over the next twelve calendar months. No item is represented as a funded commitment, assigned person or implemented feature.

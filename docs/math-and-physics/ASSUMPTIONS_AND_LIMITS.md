# Assumptions and limits

> Documentation snapshot: **2026-11-19**. Implementation examined: [`0304bfec25c43f42f103ebda239749b88609a187`](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-power-models/tree/0304bfec25c43f42f103ebda239749b88609a187). Execution evidence and proposals are identified separately.

## What the models establish

The native implementation demonstrates a constant-parameter lumped thermal balance and two ideal capacitor relationships. The browser provides deterministic teaching proxies. They support equation inspection, software regression, classroom exercises and early comparisons within their stated assumptions. They do not establish a measured device model, battery safety envelope, control-system design, lifetime forecast or hardware qualification. [Native model boundary](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-power-models/blob/0304bfec25c43f42f103ebda239749b88609a187/README.md#L134-L157), [native expressions](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-power-models/blob/0304bfec25c43f42f103ebda239749b88609a187/src/battery_thermal.py#L48-L55), [DC-link expressions](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-power-models/blob/0304bfec25c43f42f103ebda239749b88609a187/src/dc_link.py#L9-L44), [browser calibration declaration](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-power-models/blob/0304bfec25c43f42f103ebda239749b88609a187/docs/lesson-config.json#L48-L134).

## Physical assumptions by calculation

| Calculation | Assumptions actually used | Missing effects / information needed before extending use |
|---|---|---|
| Native thermal | One uniform temperature; fixed R, C_th, G and ambient; I held constant per supplied interval; I²R heating only | Spatial gradients, cell geometry, state/temperature-dependent resistance, reversible heat, thermal contact details, radiation, phase change, temperature-dependent properties, chemistry and measured calibration |
| Active browser thermal | Constant direct Q; ambient initial state; linear conductance; same closed-form first-order response | Native current/resistance mapping and custom initial temperature; lesson limits are slider bounds, not physical validity bounds |
| Triangular ripple | Ideal capacitor; symmetric zero-mean triangular capacitor current at specified frequency | ESR/ESL, current-rating and thermal limits, DC bias/tolerance/aging, topology-specific current derivation and transient control response |
| Energy pulse | Ideal capacitor supplies constant power over the pulse; positive voltage drop below initial bus voltage | Source replenishment, converter efficiency/losses, current limits, allowable minimum voltage under control dynamics, capacitor ratings and uncertainty |

Sources: [thermal](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-power-models/blob/0304bfec25c43f42f103ebda239749b88609a187/src/battery_thermal.py#L14-L76), [MATLAB thermal](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-power-models/blob/0304bfec25c43f42f103ebda239749b88609a187/matlab/battery_thermal_lumped.m#L1-L49), [browser](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-power-models/blob/0304bfec25c43f42f103ebda239749b88609a187/docs/index.html#L244-L267), [DC-link](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-power-models/blob/0304bfec25c43f42f103ebda239749b88609a187/src/dc_link.py#L9-L44). Missing effects above follow from the absence of corresponding terms/states in these implementations; they are not newly discovered hardware faults.

## Input domain is not a validated operating envelope

Native validation checks finite scalars and mathematical sign constraints. It accepts finite ambient and initial temperatures without a physical absolute-zero bound, accepts arbitrary finite signed currents until arithmetic becomes unrepresentable, and requires positive conductance. It neither enforces electrochemical limits nor verifies that a lumped approximation is adequate. Choosing extreme but accepted inputs does not make their interpretation physical. [Native validation](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-power-models/blob/0304bfec25c43f42f103ebda239749b88609a187/src/battery_thermal.py#L21-L46), [initial-state validation](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-power-models/blob/0304bfec25c43f42f103ebda239749b88609a187/src/battery_thermal.py#L65-L71).

The implementation provides no adaptive solver, error estimator or mesh. The analytical update is exact for the held-input differential equation in real arithmetic; machine arithmetic and a piecewise-constant approximation to a changing input remain sources of error. Large steps are stable for this equation, but may misrepresent unresolved changes in a real input profile. The thermal expression can round tiny increments away. DC-link arithmetic rejects several extreme-scale overflow/underflow cases rather than returning physically misleading zeros. [Update](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-power-models/blob/0304bfec25c43f42f103ebda239749b88609a187/src/battery_thermal.py#L48-L55), [bounds tests](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-power-models/blob/0304bfec25c43f42f103ebda239749b88609a187/tests/test_numeric_boundaries.py#L1-L118).

## Allocation and output limits

`constant_profile` and `simulate` keep full lists in memory; neither is a streaming solver. A count below `sys.maxsize` can still exceed available RAM. `write_csv` materializes its current iterable, rounds temperatures to six decimals and overwrites an existing valid destination. CSV rounding is a presentation choice, not the internal model precision. [Allocation](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-power-models/blob/0304bfec25c43f42f103ebda239749b88609a187/src/battery_thermal.py#L59-L113).

Browser thermal resolution is a fixed 241-point display/export series over 60 minutes, while individual checkpoints are evaluated directly at their specified times. The curve's sampling is not an adaptive numerical integration. Browser export and native CSV have different fields, units and sampling conventions. The browser library includes nine other teaching kinds; their presence is not evidence of native implementations or validation of those domains. [Browser controller](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-power-models/blob/0304bfec25c43f42f103ebda239749b88609a187/docs/index.html#L339-L487), [catalog](../developer/BROWSER_MODEL_CATALOG.md).

## Evidence limits

The frozen Python/MATLAB fixture says its values originate in Python baseline execution. The current capture/verify helper adds fresh Python execution and source hashes. MATLAB parity requires actual MATLAB execution; the audit's local runtime did not have it. A successful exact-source historical GitHub MATLAB check was separately observed, but it does not establish a later candidate's result. [Fixture](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-power-models/blob/0304bfec25c43f42f103ebda239749b88609a187/tests/fixtures/python_matlab_parity.json#L1-L18), [MATLAB gate](https://github.com/mohammadrezwankhan/battery-power-models/blob/0304bfec25c43f42f103ebda239749b88609a187/tools/security_numerical_gate.m#L1-L116), [verification ledger](../maintainer/VERIFICATION_EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md).

The locally observed 100% numerical line/branch coverage covers the executed test paths, including bounded generated inputs. It does not prove all finite floating-point cases, every Python/MATLAB version, a minimal dependency environment, empirical model accuracy or browser usability. Security scanner internals and generated artwork are honestly indexed where they were not exhaustively analyzed; see [source accounting](../SOURCE_EVIDENCE.md).

## Extending the evidence responsibly

A future calibrated model should identify the device and measurement method, license/provenance of data, parameter-fitting procedure, train/validation separation, uncertainty, operating range and out-of-sample error. A future coupled controller should specify state, sign conventions, saturations, sample/hold behavior, solver and stability checks. These are proposed research/engineering work in the [roadmap](../../ROADMAP.md), not capabilities delivered by this documentation change.
